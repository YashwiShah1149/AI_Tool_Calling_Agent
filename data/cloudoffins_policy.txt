Cloudoffis Employee Handbook & Internal Knowledge Base
Company Overview
Cloudoffis is a technology company focused on cloud automation, AI-powered document processing, workflow
optimization, and enterprise software solutions.
Core company values:- Ownership- Transparency- Continuous Learning- Automation First- Customer Obsession- Fast Execution
Standard office working hours: 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST.
-------------------------------------------
Leave Policy
Casual Leave- 12 casual leaves per year- Maximum 3 consecutive casual leaves allowed without approval- Unused casual leaves cannot be carried forward
Sick Leave- 10 sick leaves per year- Medical certificate required beyond 3 consecutive days
Earned Leave- Eligible after 12 months- Carry forward allowed up to 20 days
-------------------------------------------
Work From Home Policy- 
Hybrid work model- Minimum 3 office days per week- Tuesday and Thursday are mandatory office days- Work from home not allowed during client audit weeks without approval
Permanent remote work may be approved for:- Medical reasons- Relocation- International employees
-------------------------------------------
Attendance Policy- 
Employees arriving after 10:00 AM are marked late- 3 late marks convert into half-day leave deduction- Minimum 8 working hours required daily
-------------------------------------------
Laptop & Security Policy- 
Office laptops must not be shared- Password protection mandatory- Confidential client files must not be uploaded to personal cloud storage- Public Wi-Fi should not be used without VPN
Password Rules:- Minimum 10 characters- Must include uppercase, lowercase, number, special character- Password reset every 90 days
-------------------------------------------
AI Usage Policy
Approved:- Email drafting- Meeting summaries- Documentation generation- Code assistance
Restricted:- Uploading confidential client documents to public AI tools- Sharing passwords with AI systems- Generating fake invoices or forged signatures
Employees must review AI-generated content before sharing externally.
-------------------------------------------
Client Communication Guidelines
Employees must:- Use professional language- Avoid slang- Escalate critical issues immediately- Never commit unrealistic timelines
Immediate escalation required for:- Production outages- Security incidents- Legal notices- Client data leaks
-------------------------------------------
Performance Review Process
Reviews occur twice yearly.
Evaluation criteria:- Technical skills- Ownership- Communication- Team collaboration- Delivery consistency
High performers may receive:- Promotions- Leadership opportunities- Performance bonuses
-------------------------------------------
Software Development Guidelines
Coding standards:- Use meaningful variable names- Avoid hardcoding secrets- Write modular functions- Add comments for complex logic
Pull Request Rules:
- At least one reviewer required- Automated tests must pass
Deployment Policy:- Production deployments allowed Monday to Thursday- Allowed between 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST- Friday deployments require special approval
-------------------------------------------
SMSF Audit Department Knowledge Base
Common documents:- Trust Deeds- Financial Statements- Investment Reports- Bank Statements- ASIC Company Extracts
Common audit risks:- Missing signatures- Unsupported valuations- Missing confirmations- Incorrect balances
Escalate immediately if:- Fraud indicators found- Regulatory breaches suspected
-------------------------------------------
Internal IT Support Knowledge Base
VPN Troubleshooting:
1. Restart VPN client
2. Check internet
3. Re-enter credentials
4. Contact IT support
Laptop replacement eligibility:- After 4 years unless severe hardware failure
Restricted software:
- Pirated software- Unauthorized remote access tools- Cryptocurrency mining applications
-------------------------------------------
Employee Travel Policy
Reimbursement claims must be submitted within 15 days.
Eligible expenses:- Flights- Hotels- Local transport- Client meals
Non-reimbursable:- Personal shopping- Entertainment expenses- Alcohol purchases
-------------------------------------------
Learning & Development Policy
Employees may receive reimbursement for:- Online certifications- Technical workshops- Industry conferences
Conditions:- Manager approval required- Proof of completion required
Maximum yearly reimbursement:
INR 25,000

Internship Program Guidelines
Interns are expected to:- Attend daily standups- Submit weekly updates
- Maintain documentation- Ask questions actively
Evaluation criteria:- Technical growth- Communication- Initiative- Ownership
Outstanding interns may receive full-time offers
