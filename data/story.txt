The Tortoise and the Rabbit 


Once upon a time, a speedy rabbit lived in a forest. The rabbit was very proud of how fast he could 
run. Every day, he would laugh at a slow-moving tortoise. 
One day, the rabbit said to the tortoise, “You are so slow. I could finish a race before you even move 
halfway.” 
The tortoise calmly replied, “Speed is not everything. Let us have a race and see who wins.” 
All the animals in the forest gathered to watch the race. A fox marked the starting and finishing lines. 
As soon as the race began, the rabbit ran very quickly and soon disappeared far ahead of the 
tortoise. The tortoise continued walking slowly but steadily. 
After some time, the rabbit looked back and saw that the tortoise was still far behind. Feeling 
overconfident, the rabbit decided to rest under a tree. 
“I have plenty of time,” thought the rabbit. “I will take a short nap.” 
The rabbit soon fell asleep. Meanwhile, the tortoise kept moving slowly without stopping. 
Hours later, the rabbit woke up suddenly and realized the tortoise was close to the finish line. The 
rabbit ran as fast as he could, but it was too late. 
The tortoise crossed the finish line first and won the race. 
All the animals cheered for the tortoise. The rabbit felt embarrassed and understood his mistake. 
The tortoise smiled and said, “Slow and steady wins the race.” 
Moral of the story: 
Consistency, patience, and hard work are more important than overconfidence.
